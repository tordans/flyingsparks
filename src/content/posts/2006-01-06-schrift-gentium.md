<a href="http://scripts.sil.org/cms/scripts/page.php?site_id=nrsi&#038;id=Gentium">Gentium</a>


<blockquote>Gentium is a typeface family designed to enable the diverse ethnic groups around the world who use the Latin script to produce readable, high-quality publications. It supports a wide range of Latin-based alphabets and includes glyphs that correspond to all the Latin ranges of Unicode.</blockquote>


Eine sehr schöne und hervorragend ausgebaute Serif-Schirft, die bestimmt in einem nächsten Projekt zum Einsatz kommt :).
