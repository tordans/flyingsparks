<p align="center"><a href="/wp-content/uploads/digglabs.jpg"></a> </p>
<p>Wer sie -- wie ich bisher :) -- noch nicht kennt, sollte sich diese <strong>drei schönen Datenvisualisierungen aus den Digg Labs</strong> anschauen.</p>
<p>Auf ganz unterschiedliche Arten, werden die aktuell gediggten Stories dargestellt. Zusammen mit ein paar Metainformationen.</p>
<p>(Die Links auf den Bildern vergrößern das Thumbnail.)</p>
<p align="center"><a href="/wp-content/uploads/digglabsswarm.jpg"><img height="209" alt="digg labs swarm" src="/wp-content/uploads/digglabsswarm-small.jpg" width="450" /></a><br />
<a href="http://labs.digg.com/swarm/">http://labs.digg.com/swarm/</a><br />
 <em>Digg Swarm draws a circle for stories as they're dugg. Diggers swarm around stories, and make them grow. Brightly colored stories have more diggs.</em></p>
<p align="center"><a href="/wp-content/uploads/digglabsbigspy.jpg"><img height="209" alt="digg labs bigspy" src="/wp-content/uploads/digglabsbigspy-small.jpg" width="450" /></a><br />
<a href="http://labs.digg.com/stack/http://labs.digg.com/bigspy/"><u><font color="blue">http://labs.digg.com/bigspy/</font></u></a><br />
<em>Digg BigSpy places stories at the top of the screen as they are dugg. As new stories are dugg, older stories move down the list. Bigger stories have more diggs.</em></p>
<p align="center"><a href="/wp-content/uploads/digglabsstack.jpg"><img height="209" alt="digg labs stack" src="/wp-content/uploads/digglabsstack-small.jpg" width="450" /></a><br />
<a href="http://labs.digg.com/stack/">http://labs.digg.com/stack/</a><br />
 <em>Digg Stack shows diggs occurring in real time on up to 100 stories at once. Diggers fall from above and stack up on popular stories. Brightly colored stories have more diggs.</em></p>
